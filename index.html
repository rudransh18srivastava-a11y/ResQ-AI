AI Risk Assessment

The ResQ Risk Engine combines live weather forecasts and recent seismic activity to score flood, storm, heatwave, wildfire and earthquake risk for any place.

–

Scores come from a transparent rule-based model on forecast data (Open-Meteo) and USGS earthquakes. Always follow official warnings from IMD, NDMA and your state authorities.

Request Emergency Help

Fill this in and ResQ captures your location, ranks the urgency with AI triage and shows it on the responder dashboard. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 first.

What happens next

  1. Location captured from your device GPS.
  2. AI triage scores urgency from keywords, people count and type.
  3. Responders see it on the dashboard, sorted by priority.
  4. Share it via WhatsApp or SMS so family can reach you.

While you wait

  • Stay calm, keep your phone charged and in power-saving mode.
  • Move to a safe, high or open place if it is safe to do so.
  • Keep ID, medicines, water and a torch within reach.
  • Signal with a whistle, torch or bright cloth.

How ResQ works

🧠

🚑

Admins can update status, delete requests, load demo data and export. Everyone else has view-only access.
